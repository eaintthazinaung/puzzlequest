Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PuzzleQuest

How do I play PuzzleQuest?➕

Choose a category → select an image → pick a grid size (3×3 to 7×7) → click tiles to slide them and reconstruct the image. Complete the puzzle within the move limit to win!

What happens when I complete a puzzle?➕

You'll see your results (moves, time, points earned) and unlock a Knowledge Card with an interesting fact about the image. The card is saved to your Library forever!

Can I restart a puzzle mid-game?➕

Yes! Click the 🔀 Restart button during a game. A confirmation dialog will appear, and if you confirm, the tiles will be reshuffled and your moves and timer will reset. No life is lost for restarting.

How does the life system work?➕

You start with 5 lives. A life is lost only if you exceed the move limit. Lives automatically refill one per minute up to the maximum of 5. You can also buy lives with 10 points each.

What are the move limits for each grid size?➕
  • 3×3 → 100 moves
  • 4×4 → 200 moves
  • 5×5 → 300 moves
  • 6×6 → 400 moves
  • 7×7 → 500 moves
How many points do I earn per puzzle?➕
  • 3×3 → 10 pts
  • 4×4 → 15 pts
  • 5×5 → 25 pts
  • 6×6 → 35 pts
  • 7×7 → 50 pts
What can I do with points?➕

Points appear on the leaderboard. You can also spend them on extra lives — 10 points per life — from your Profile page.

How do I create an account?➕

Click "Login" in the navigation, switch to the Register tab, choose a unique player name, and select an avatar emoji. Your progress is saved locally on this device.

Can I have multiple profiles?➕

Yes! Click "➕ New Profile" on your Profile page to create another account. Switch between profiles from the "Switch Profile" section.

How do I delete my account?➕

Go to your Profile page and click "🗑️ Delete Account". Confirm in the dialog that appears. This permanently removes all your data and cannot be undone.

Is my data saved in the cloud?➕

All data is stored in your browser's LocalStorage. Progress stays on this device only. Clearing your browser data will erase your progress.

Can I play on mobile?➕

Absolutely! PuzzleQuest is fully responsive and works great on smartphones and tablets. The grid automatically adjusts to fit your screen.
