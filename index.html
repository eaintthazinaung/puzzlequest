📖 The Legend of Aetheria

Aetheria was once a world of color, where every shade was born from the memories of its people. But when a mysterious force known as "The Great Gray" erased those memories, the world faded into silence—leaving everything broken, empty, and gray.

High above it all, the "Lost Atelier" still remains—a forgotten place where memories can be restored. There, a lonely owl named "Barnaby" waits for someone who can bring the colors back.

"That someone is you."

Guided by a mysterious message, you will choose a Guardian spirit and journey across shattered worlds, solving puzzles to recover what was lost. With every memory you restore, a piece of Aetheria comes back to life.
