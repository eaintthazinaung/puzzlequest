Behind the Game

About PuzzleQuest

PuzzleQuest is a solo-developed puzzle experience that combines creativity, logic, and learning into one colorful adventure.

🧩 My Mission

I created PuzzleQuest to make learning more interactive and fun. Every completed puzzle unlocks a fascinating fact — because games should challenge your brain and expand your knowledge at the same time.

Meet the Creator

Eaint Thazin Aung

I designed and developed PuzzleQuest independently using pure HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. As a puzzle enthusiast, I wanted to build something simple yet meaningful — a game that blends entertainment with education. Every feature, animation, and knowledge card was carefully crafted to create an enjoyable user experience.

💡 Why PuzzleQuest?

I believe learning doesn’t have to feel like studying. By combining image sliding puzzles with interesting facts, PuzzleQuest transforms simple gameplay into a small journey of discovery.
